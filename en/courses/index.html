Courses

I offer one-on-one online tutoring in the following subjects. All sessions are conducted in English or Mandarin, tailored to each student's pace and goals.

AP Calculus AB / BC

AP Calculus is one of the most demanding and rewarding math courses in high school. I help students build a solid conceptual foundation — limits, derivatives, integrals, and series — while developing the problem-solving techniques needed to succeed on the AP exam.

Whether you're aiming for a 4 or a 5, or simply trying to keep up with a fast-moving class, I tailor each session to where you are and where you need to go.

AP Precalculus

AP Precalculus covers functions, trigonometry, and mathematical modeling — concepts that form the foundation for calculus and beyond. I focus on building real fluency with these topics so students are well-prepared for AP Calculus and college-level math.

AP Statistics

Statistics is everywhere — in science, economics, medicine, and everyday life. In AP Statistics, I help students develop a clear understanding of data analysis, probability, and inference, with an emphasis on reasoning rather than memorization.

My background in statistics (master's and PhD level) means I can explain these concepts from first principles, not just teach to the test.

AP Physics 1

AP Physics 1 introduces algebra-based mechanics, waves, and circuits. Many students find physics unintuitive at first — I help them connect the math to physical reasoning, so the concepts actually make sense rather than feeling like formulas to memorize.

AP Computer Science A

AP Computer Science A teaches object-oriented programming in Java. With an undergraduate degree in Computer Science, I bring real depth to this subject — covering not just what the code does, but why it works the way it does.

I help students build strong programming intuition and prepare effectively for the AP exam.

Regents Algebra I & II

The New York State Regents exams in Algebra are a key milestone for high school students. I help students master the core concepts, work through past exam questions, and build the confidence they need on test day.
