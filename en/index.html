Who Am I

I hold a PhD in Applied Mathematics and have extensive experience in teaching. During both my master’s and doctoral studies, I worked as a teaching assistant, teaching undergraduate-level courses. In recent years, I have focused on one-on-one tutoring for high school students in mathematics and physics. My teaching focuses on building a strong foundation. I make sure each concept is clearly understood and guide students step by step through carefully selected problems. I also emphasize developing good study habits, especially clear and structured note-taking, so that students can truly internalize what they learn and build lasting learning skills.
